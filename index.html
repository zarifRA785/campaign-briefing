The rating is how much traction a deal is getting now: activity a day over the last 3 days. Very popular is 50 or more a day, Popular 15 or more, Doing okay 3 or more, Not doing well half a point or more, Doing badly less. Peaked shows a deal's busiest stretch of about 3 days that we watched, when it was two or more words higher than now; where we have too little history, past its peak compares with its whole-run average instead.
