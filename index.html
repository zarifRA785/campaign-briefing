Activity is how busy a deal is: up votes minus down votes, plus twice the comments, plus one point per 50 clicks. Very popular is 50 or more a day, Popular 15 or more, Doing okay 3 or more, Not doing well half a point or more, Doing badly less. Peaked shows a deal's busiest stretch of about 3 days that we watched, when it was two or more words higher than now; where we have too little history, past its peak compares with its whole-run average instead.
