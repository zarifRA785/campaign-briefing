The words on every card

Hit
At least a tenth of the comments say they bought it.
Backlash
Lots of down votes, or at least a quarter of the comments angry about the deal or the store. The product itself never counts.

In between, every 6 hours, a quick look finds new deals and checks the numbers of those in their first two days, when most deals do most of their work. Back in stock marks a deal that sold out and came back, Live again one that ended and re-opened; both are watched again.

Activity is how busy a deal is: up votes minus down votes, plus twice the comments, plus one point per 50 clicks. Votes and comments count most because they show someone cared; a click only shows they looked. In All campaigns, hover over a deal's activity or open More to see its sum; the column before it is what it gained since yesterday. On a card, each figure shows what it gained, and the line under them how much activity that adds up to. Since yesterday means since the check closest to 24 hours earlier; when that check is more than three hours off a day, the report says the hours instead.

The rating is how much traction a deal is getting now: activity a day over the last 3 days. Very popular is 50 or more a day, Popular 15 or more, Doing okay 3 or more, Not doing well half a point or more, Doing badly less. Peaked shows a deal's busiest stretch of about 3 days that we watched, when it was two or more words higher than now; where we have too little history, past its peak compares with its whole-run average instead.

The person who posted the deal is often the store or a fan, so their comments are never counted or quoted as a customer's.
